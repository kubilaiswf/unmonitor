Unmonitor

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Unmonitor doesn't collect, store or send any personal data. It never connects to the internet and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

The only data it keeps is its own settings, such as which display is your speaker and which features are turned on. They are saved in a file on your PC, in %APPDATA%\Unmonitor\config.ini, and never leave it.

To read and change displays, windows, the mouse position and the audio device, Unmonitor uses Windows features that run only on your PC.

Questions: github.com/kubilaiswf/unmonitor/issues
